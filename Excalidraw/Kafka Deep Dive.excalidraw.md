Terminology

Broker: The servers that hold the queue

Partition: The "queue". An ordered, immutable sequence of message that we append to, like a log file. Each broker can have multiple partitions

Topic: A logical grouping of partitions. You publish to and consume from topics in kafka.

Producers: Write messages to topic

Consumers: Read messages off of topics ^M4A7YTXY

Importantly, you can use Kafka as either a message queue or a stream. Frankly, the distinction here is minor. In both modes, consumers track their progress using offset commits.  ^KFNiqbbF
